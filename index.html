The Ocean Needs Kaitiaki

In Aotearoa, the concept of kaitiakitanga means guardianship, stewardship, and protection.

Our relationship with the moana (ocean) is deeply connected—it provides the oxygen we breathe, regulates our global climate, and sustains thousands of marine species.

True kaitiakitanga isn't just about admiring the ocean; it is an active commitment to restore balance (mauri) where human impact has caused harm.

By taking care of our coastal waters today, we uphold our role as guardians to ensure our oceans thrive for generations to come.
