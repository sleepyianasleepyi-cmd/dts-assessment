ACTIONS: Becoming a Kaitiaki for Our Moana

Kaitiakitanga is a practice—it requires active involvement.

Here is how you can step up as a guardian for our coastal waters and marine life.

Everyday Individual Actions

1. Everyday Actions at Home & School

Small daily choices add up to make a huge difference for marine ecosystems.

Reduce Single-Use Plastics: Swap out plastic water bottles, grocery bags, cutlery, and straws for reusable alternatives to prevent waste from entering waterways.

Dispose of Waste Properly: Always recycle and ensure trash is secure so it doesn't blow into storm drains, which flow straight out to sea.

Choose Eco-Friendly Products: Use biodegradable cleaning products and non-toxic sunscreen to protect marine habitats from harmful chemical runoff.

Conserve Water: Using less water reduces the amount of runoff and wastewater that flows back into ocean environments.


Community & Coastal Protection

2. Getting Involved in Your Community

Protecting our ocean is more powerful when we work together as a community.

Participate in Local Beach Cleanups: Join or organize community beach and river cleanups to collect trash before it breaks down into microplastics.

Plant Native Coastal Trees: Help restore sand dunes and stream banks by planting native vegetation, which prevents soil erosion and filters runoff before it reaches the sea.

Support Sustainable Seafood: Make informed purchasing choices by choosing sustainably caught fish to encourage responsible fishing practices.


Advocacy & Support

3. Advocacy & Conservation Support

Raising your voice and supporting conservation efforts helps create long-term systemic change.

Support Marine Reserves & Protected Areas: Advocate for the creation and expansion of marine protected areas where fish, kelp forests, and coral reefs can safely recover.

Spread Ocean Awareness: Use your platform, school projects, or social media to educate others about the importance of kaitiakitanga and ocean conservation.

Support Conservation Groups: Donate time or resources to local environmental initiatives working to protect coastal environments.


The Kaitiaki Pledge

"I pledge to respect, protect, and care for our moana. I will reduce my waste, make conscious choices, and act as a guardian for marine life today and for future generations."
